Player privacy

Guest and account scores

Minute Arcade uses a session cookie to recognize your player and stores earned Arcade Points on its server. Your browser also keeps game records, settings and unfinished scores. Clearing your browser data can disconnect a guest from their points.

Your account

Signing up requires a username and password. A nickname is optional. The arcade stores a salted password verifier. You can change your nickname and score visibility on your account page. Keep your password somewhere safe because the arcade does not collect a recovery email.

Who can see scores

New accounts are private. Opting in shows your username, nickname and score on the leaderboard and in friend comparisons. You can switch back to private at any time. A challenge link you choose to send may contain that game’s result. Dial Relay links include the clue you wrote.

Advertising

The arcade has banner locations on its home and game screens. When Google advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses information from partner sites.
